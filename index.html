Executive Insights & Recommendations

A board-ready narrative generated from the acquisition, adoption, retention, and conversion views.

Live model

Executive summary · live scenario

Healthy acquisition, with a clear activation opportunity.

Sprintly is growing its customer base while sustaining solid project activity. Starter accounts consistently outperform free-tier accounts on engagement and completion, making targeted onboarding and timely upgrade prompts the strongest near-term growth levers.

Key Insights

What the operating data is telling us

  1. 1
    Strong product adoption among paid customers

    Starter customers maintain higher project creation and login frequency, indicating deeper habit formation after upgrade.

  2. 2
    Free-to-paid conversion can move materially

    High-activity free accounts resemble paid cohorts but convert at only 18.3%, leaving a focused growth opportunity.

  3. 3
    Partner acquisition brings durable value

    Partner-sourced customers lead engagement and conversion, even at lower overall volume.

  4. 4
    Risk is concentrated in low-activity accounts

    Churn signals cluster around weak login cadence, limited collaboration, and incomplete onboarding.

  5. 5
    Power users reveal the expansion path

    Accounts with more projects and collaborators show the strongest retention and upgrade readiness.

Recommended Actions

A prioritized client action plan

  1. 1
    Launch a free-to-paid upgrade campaign

    Target highly engaged free accounts with usage-based messages, time-boxed trials, and proof of team value.

  2. 2
    Strengthen product onboarding

    Guide customers to create their first project, invite collaborators, and complete the critical activation sequence.

  3. 3
    Prioritize high-performing channels

    Increase investment in partner and referral sources while maintaining organic acquisition efficiency.

  4. 4
    Establish a customer health monitor

    Trigger proactive outreach when login cadence, active days, and completion fall below healthy benchmarks.

  5. 5
    Promote collaborative workflows

    Encourage team invitations and shared project milestones to deepen adoption and reduce single-user risk.
